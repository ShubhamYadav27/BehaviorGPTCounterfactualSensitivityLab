Technical Distinction: Static Item Similarity vs. Behavioral Trajectory Prediction
client.similar_items() computes static pairwise nearest neighbors directly from the catalog's latent embeddings. In contrast, client.complete(history) processes a multi-action behavioral trajectory through transformer attention, dynamically shifting recommendations based on sequential momentum, action recency, and cross-category transitions that static pairwise similarity cannot represent.
